# doing what matters

I had been pondering over this for quite sometime now, about how a man can be truly productive. What struck me was when I started to reflect on my own self to analyze how I act throughout the day and my habits!

reasons why I feel I am not as productive as I can be:
- overthinking and not doing what matters - I tend to overthink a lot (think about the future, or the past) not realizing the current time is more precious than anything. What I get done at the present is all that matters.
- doing the things that really matters - this is something that is difficult for me as I am too OCD about that perfect workflow or that perfect conditions to work, which is practically impossible and one of the reasons I am not as productive as I can be!
