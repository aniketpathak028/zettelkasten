# OCD and how I am trying to overcome it!

I have a severe OCD condition where I try being the absolute perfect at everything I do, which is severely hampering my mental health and productivity!

Why??
	- I don't admit the fact that I can never be perfect and chaos is the state nature desires! 🙂
	- no matter how much we manage things, they are bound to be at chaos and learning to live through chaos is a beauty in itself...

## how I plan to overcome OCD?

- forcing myself to live in chaos
- appreciating the chaos and learning how things are perfect even when they are at chaos
- making myself realize nothing really matters!
- focus on achieving the target rather than perfecting things that do not lead me to my goal but are merely cosmetic
