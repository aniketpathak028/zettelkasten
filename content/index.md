Welcome to my Zettelkasten! 👋

Here I share everything I’m learning—whether it’s tech insights, language adventures, or late-night philosophical musings.


[Zettelkasten](https://zettelkasten.de/overview/) is a German term meaning "slip box"—a powerful note-taking system designed to help you connect ideas and deepen your understanding.

### Why?
I mostly made this for myself as I realized my note taking was extremely poor and I kept forgetting stuff after a while.. sometimes when I did have notes they weren't organized and collectively present at the same place! The idea was to start a zettelkasten of my own and host it on the internet, so that my notes are not just publicly available to me and others but also organized at a single place for reference. 😇

Here I plan on sharing everything I’m learning—whether it’s tech insights, language adventures (currently  I have been learning German 🇩🇪 for a while), or late-night philosophical musings.

I tend to use the [[para]] method to organize my notes, it something that seems to work well for me, do checkout if you want to read more about it!

If you enjoy exploring new ideas and making connections, feel free to bookmark this page or add it to your favorite [RSS](index.xml) reader to stay updated. ✌️

~aniket

PS: Some notes might be incorrect or have missing information as I often tend to keep updating it as a part of my note-taking habit.
